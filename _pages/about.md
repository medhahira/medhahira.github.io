I am Medha Hira, a Master's student in Computer Science & Natural Language Processing at the [Language Technologies Institute](https://lti.cs.cmu.edu/), School of Computer Science, Carnegie Mellon University. I am advised by [Dr. Maarten Sap](https://maartensap.com/), and my current research focuses on retrieval for code-switched and low-resource languages.

Before CMU, I completed my B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering at the Indraprastha Institute of Information Technology, Delhi, where I was advised by [Dr. Anubha Gupta](https://www.iiitd.ac.in/anubha) and [Dr. Rajiv Ratn Shah](https://www.iiitd.edu.in/~rajivratn/), as well as [Dr. Sameep Mehta](https://research.ibm.com/people/sameep-mehta) and [Dr. Nishtha Madaan](https://nishthaa.github.io/) at IBM Research India.

Since 2023 I have worked across Large Language Models, Natural Language Processing, and Speech Processing. I am particularly interested in model safety, multilingual and multicultural communication, agentic reasoning, and emotion understanding in speech.

Alongside my academic work, I have interned as a Software Engineer (ML) at Google, a Research Intern at Microsoft Research India with [Dr. Akshay Nambi](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/people/akshayn/), and a Data Scientist at SAP, U.A.E.
