I'm interested in making language technology work for the way people actually speak — across languages, registers, and code-switched settings. My current research focuses on retrieval for code-switched and low-resource languages. More broadly I work on model safety, multilingual and multicultural communication, agentic reasoning, and emotion understanding in speech.

Before CMU I completed my B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering at [IIIT-Delhi](https://www.iiitd.ac.in/), where I was advised by [Dr. Anubha Gupta](https://www.iiitd.ac.in/anubha) and [Dr. Rajiv Ratn Shah](https://www.iiitd.edu.in/~rajivratn/), and worked with [Dr. Sameep Mehta](https://research.ibm.com/people/sameep-mehta) and [Dr. Nishtha Madaan](https://nishthaa.github.io/) at IBM Research India.

I've also spent time as a Software Engineer (ML) Intern at Google, a Research Intern at Microsoft Research India with [Dr. Akshay Nambi](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/people/akshayn/), and a Data Science Intern at SAP in Dubai. Please reach out if you'd like to chat — I enjoy talking about research.
